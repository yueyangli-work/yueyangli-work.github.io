Fudan University · Environmental Science

Yueyang Li

Aerosol science and instrumentation development

I am a doctoral researcher in the Department of Environmental Science and Engineering at Fudan University, advised by Prof. Lin Wang. My research focuses on the physics and chemistry of aerosol nanoparticles and on developing instruments to generate, charge, classify, and characterize them.

View publications ↓

Research interests

Aerosol Physics and Chemistry
Sub-10 nm particle charging, size-resolved chemical characterization of ions and clusters, and atmospheric new particle formation.
Controlled Aerosol Generation and Nanoparticle Engineering
Electrospray aerosol generation, metal nanoparticle generation, and size classification of 1–3 nm particles.
Aerosol Instrumentation and Measurement
Dielectric barrier discharge chargers, electrical mobility analysis, and online measurements of cluster size and composition.
Pharmaceutical Aerosol Formulation and Inhaled Drug Delivery
Emerging interest in applying particle generation and characterization to inhalable formulations.

Research instruments

P5-DMA-MS for simultaneous size and composition measurements

I integrated a dielectric barrier discharge (DBD) charger, a modified P5 differential mobility analyzer (P5-DMA), and an atmospheric pressure interface time-of-flight mass spectrometer (APi-TOF-MS) to measure the size distribution and chemical composition of atmospheric clusters in a single measurement workflow.

High-efficiency unipolar DBD charger

I developed and evaluated a unipolar aerosol charger for measuring sub-10 nm particles.

Bipolar charging and ion characterization
Characterized the electrical mobility and chemical composition of ions from a bipolar DBD charger. Li et al. (2025) ↗
Field measurements and mass spectrometry
Experience with APi-TOF, CIMS, and Vocus PTR-TOF. Coordinated instrument deployment at the Dianshan Lake supersite and maintained mass spectrometry and particle sizing instruments during field campaigns.

Research projects

High-Resolution Size and Composition Analysis of Atmospheric Clusters

NSFC National Major Scientific Instrument Development Project · Grant 22127811

Key participant · Approved in 2021 · Grant term 2022–2026

Developed electrospray and metal nanoparticle generation modules for a system that classifies 1–3 nm particles. Developed a high-efficiency unipolar particle charger and contributed to integrating the instruments for online cluster measurements.

See the integrated P5-DMA-MS system ↑

Atmospheric Oxidation and Secondary Particle Formation

Shanghai Municipal Bureau of Ecology and Environment · Key participant · 2021–2022

Coordinated field measurements at the Dianshan Lake supersite, analyzed particle size distributions from 1 to 700 nm during new particle formation events, and investigated potential sources of highly oxygenated organic compounds.
